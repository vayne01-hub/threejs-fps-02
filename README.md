SABLE YARD is a round-based tactical FPS in the style Counter-Strike made famous: two teams, short rounds won by eliminating the other team or by the spike objective, money between rounds to buy weapons, and gunfire where accuracy counts more than speed. Plant the spike at A or B, hold the site until it blows, and mind the headshots — they pay triple. Fully offline, three.js 0.187.0, bots included.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts  # then open http://localhost:8080
```
